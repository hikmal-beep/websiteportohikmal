BACKGROUND SPECIFICATION
========================

The world consists of one static background image.

Image Size
1024 x 1536

Coordinate System

(0,0) = Top Left

X increases right.

Y increases downward.

The image represents an outdoor wedding venue.

Top Region
-----------
Dense forest.

Contains:

Trees
Bushes
Lanterns
Vines

Middle Region
-------------
Wedding stage.

Contains:

Stone stairs
Stone platform
Curtains
Wood wall

Lower Middle
------------
Ceremony floor.

Contains:

Stone tiles
Red carpet

River
-----
Blue river separating north and south.

Bridge
------
Wooden bridge connecting both sides.

Lower Region
------------
Garden.

Contains:

Grass
Flowers
Rocks
Entrance arch
Fence

This image never changes.

Nothing in the background moves.

Player and NPCs are rendered above this image.
