WORLD RULES
===========

RULE 1

The player never walks on water.

RULE 2

Trees always block movement.

RULE 3

Bushes block movement.

RULE 4

The bridge is the ONLY river crossing.

RULE 5

Player cannot leave the visible map.

RULE 6

Grass is walkable.

RULE 7

Stone floor is walkable.

RULE 8

Red carpet is walkable.

RULE 9

Fence blocks movement.

RULE 10

Rocks block movement.

RULE 11

Stage wall blocks movement.

RULE 12

Bridge overrides river collision.

RULE 13

Player collision uses the feet position instead of the entire sprite.

RULE 14

Movement should stop before entering blocked objects.

RULE 15

NPCs obey exactly the same collision rules.

RULE 16

Pathfinding must avoid blocked regions.

RULE 17

Never generate shortcuts through the river.

RULE 18

Bridge must always be selected as the crossing.

RULE 19

Background never changes.

RULE 20

No procedural terrain.
